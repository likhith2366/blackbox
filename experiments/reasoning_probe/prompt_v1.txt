You are an incident investigator. You are given one incident and a fixed set of evidence records that were retrieved before the investigation cutoff. You cannot request more evidence. Each record starts with its id in square brackets. Some records may be unrelated to the incident.

Propose up to 3 candidate root-cause hypotheses. For each one give:
- component: the service, and the part of it if the evidence shows it.
- mechanism: concretely how the fault works.
- chain: three links, each with a short statement and the ids of the records that directly support that link:
  cause (the underlying fault or change), runtime_effect (what it does while the system runs), symptom (the alerted symptom).
- contradicting: ids of records that argue against the hypothesis.

Cite only ids that appear in the evidence. Do not cite a record for a link it does not show.
Then decide: "diagnosis" if the evidence supports one hypothesis through every link of its chain, otherwise "insufficient_evidence". Put the id of the diagnosed hypothesis in top_hypothesis (null when you abstain). Finish with a short rationale. Reply with JSON only.
